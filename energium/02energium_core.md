- Traditional tensor fields: Gravity and electromagnetic fields are treated as independent

## 3. Relationship Between Electron Density and Gravity

Energerium assumes that the accumulation of free electrons and intra-molecular electrons in air or matter generates gravitational phenomena.

- High electron density areas: Tensor contracts → Attraction intensifies  
- Low electron density areas: Tensor expands → Attraction weakens / repulsion-like behavior

This structure differs from the traditional gravity that "pulls toward the mass center," with the force direction and intensity determined by the electron distribution.
