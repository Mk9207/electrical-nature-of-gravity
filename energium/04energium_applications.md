# Energium Applications

---

## [1. Antigravity Applications]

Utilizing the principle of electron density redistribution in Energium theory, this structure enables object levitation and suspension.

- **Spatial Tensor Inversion Device**  
  A mechanism that locally thins electron density to neutralize or counteract gravitational vectors. Used to generate controllable antigravity fields.

- **Levitation Control Plate**  
  A support plate that performs real-time feedback control according to tensor modulation. Applicable to stable levitation of small and medium objects.

---

## [2. Energy Conversion Applications]

Gravitational energy accumulated through spatial tensor modulation is captured as “electron density variation” and reused as current or charge.

- **Tensor Modulation → Electron Extraction Structure**  
  A multi-stage structure that collapses molecular shells via resonator, induces free electrons through magnetic alignment, and performs rectification and storage.  
  *See Core Theory Section 4: Generation and Accumulation.*

- **Gravitational Battery**  
  A compact energy capture device that naturally absorbs and stores electron deviations when placed in a tensor-modulated space. Prioritizes high reproducibility and safety.

---

## [3. Spatial Control / Shielding Structures]

Based on Energium theory, this technology manipulates or blocks spatial tensor influences to stabilize electronic fields and coordinate structures.

- **Tensor Shield Membrane**  
  A filter structure that cancels external spatial tensor interference. Isolates and protects internal space from electrostatic, magnetic, and gravitational fields.

- **Spatial Transfer Support Device**  
  A support unit that aligns local tensors and ensures base stability required for coordinate specification and transfer activation. Also designed for integration with wormhole theory.

- All of the above applications utilize “spatial tensor modulation via electron mediation,” which fundamentally differs from traditional mass-dependent energy systems.
- Key implementation considerations:
  - Precise selection of resonant frequencies

  - Optimization of rectification and storage efficiency of electron flow
