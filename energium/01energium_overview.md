## 🧭 Theory Name: Energium Theory

---

### 🔹【1】Background and Significance

The Energium Theory is a new energy theory that extends conventional gravity models (such as General Relativity and Quantum Gravity Hypotheses). It redefines gravity based on the modulation of space tensors and the electrical properties of electrons.  
The core of this theory posits that "space itself behaves as an energy field, and gravity arises locally through the density and modulation of electrons."

---

### 🔹【2】Foundational Definitions and Uniqueness

Energium theory distinctly separates itself from traditional models through the following three principles:

- **(A) Gravity = Space Structure + Electron Modulation**  
  Contrary to the classical notion that gravity arises from mass, this theory claims that gravity is generated by the accumulation and modulation of electrons, which deform the tensor structure of space.

- **(B) Variability of Space Tensors and Structural Fields**  
  Space is not uniform or isotropic; local tensor fields determine the intensity and direction of gravity. This allows for the theoretical generation of anti-gravity.

- **(C) Electrons as Fundamental Particles of Both Energy and Gravity**  
  Electrons are not just charged particles, but "space mediators" responsible for space modulation and energy transformation.
