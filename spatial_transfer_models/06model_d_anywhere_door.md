## 🔸 Component Breakdown

### ① Intent Recognition Interface

- User says where they want to go
- System displays options visually (VR, phone, etc.)
- Brainwave and eye movement analysis detects reaction and selects correct location

### ② Relay Point Calculation

- Avoids direct transfer for safety
- AI calculates a relay location to prevent spatial interference
- Safe final transfer from relay point to destination

### ③ Transfer Execution (Model C Integration)

- Model C structure applies: A (Relay) → Compressed Space → Deceleration Module → B (Destination)
- Output power adjusted between 70–100% depending on situation
- Maintains physical transfer logic and energy field compression
