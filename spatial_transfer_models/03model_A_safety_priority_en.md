# 📘 Model A: Safety-Priority Spatial Transfer Model (Reproducible with Modern Technology)

## 🔹 Purpose

This model is based on the **Future Final Form (Model C)**, with a primary focus on **reproducibility using current technology, materials, and safety standards**. Its key features are **complete transfer under C-structure protection** and a **safe landing structure via deceleration modules within the transfer path**.

## 🔹 Overall Transfer Structure Diagram (A→C→B)

`Point A (Output) → Negative Energy Space (Null Compression) → Deceleration Module Space → Point B (Arrival & Fixation)`

### 🔸 Point A (Output Mechanism)

- The transfer object is **fully enclosed by the C-structure**, shielding it from external compression.

- Point A is equipped with an **output device that compresses and nullifies the space between A and B**.

- This output compresses and aggregates the space matter from A to B at the B side, **rendering the intermediate space vacuum or sub-vacuum**.

- This intermediate space functions as a **negative energy field**.

### 🔸 Null Space Region (Negative Energy Space)

- An extremely compressed negative energy environment where **normal matter cannot exist**.

- Objects protected by the C-structure can **travel faster than light**, being drawn through the compressed space.

### 🔸 Deceleration Module Space (Intermediate Inserted Structure)

- Within the negative energy field, a **control space for deceleration** is inserted.

- Functions:

  - **Speed Control**: Gradually decelerates at intervals to **eliminate impact upon arrival at Point B**.

  - **Time Correction**: Adjusts **spacetime distortions** accumulated in the C-structure to enable precise positional control.

  - **Stability Assurance**: Cancels vibration, spin, or levitation effects via attached stabilizing modules.

### 🔸 Point B (Arrival & Fixation Mechanism)

- The transfer object, once deceleration is completed, **arrives at this endpoint**.

- Point B includes:

  - **C-Structure Release Trigger** (initiates protection removal upon arrival)

  - **Reverse Output Prevention System** (blocks counter-reaction toward Point A)

  - **Noise Removal / Null Space Convergence System**

- After arrival, **outputs are sequentially shut down**, completing fixation at Point B.

### 🔹 Fixation and Monitoring at Point A / Point B

- **Before transfer**: Fixed and monitored by holding modules at Point A.

- **After transfer**: Fixed and maintained at Point B with **repulsion suppression and retransfer prevention structures**.

## 🔹 Summary

This model is based on the premise that “spatial transfer is merely movement,” and pursues **thorough realism and safety-first design**. No warping or space deformation is involved; instead, it is completed as a structure in which objects **appear to move instantly from A to B**. As a bridge to the ultimate goal (Model C), it represents the most optimized structure at present.
