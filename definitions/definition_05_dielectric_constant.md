# Definition 05: Dielectric Constant

## Original Definition (Summary)

The dielectric constant represents how much a material electrically polarizes in response to an external electric field. Using the permittivity of vacuum (ε₀) as the reference, a material’s dielectric constant (ε) is often expressed as the relative permittivity (εᵣ). A higher dielectric constant means the material weakens the electric field more effectively.

It also relates to the effective range of Coulomb force within the material and the variation in electrostatic energy density. Dielectric constants are widely applied in capacitors, optical materials, and high-frequency circuits.
