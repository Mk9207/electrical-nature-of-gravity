# Definition 22: Radiative Pressure

## Original Definition (Condensed Summary)

Radiative pressure refers to the pressure exerted by electromagnetic radiation on a surface. It arises from the transfer of momentum from photons to matter. It is quantified using the relation:

- **P = I / c** (in the case of complete absorption)
- **P = 2I / c** (for complete reflection)

Where:
- P = Radiative pressure (N/m²)
- I = Radiation intensity (W/m²)
- c = Speed of light (≈ 3 × 10⁸ m/s)

This phenomenon plays a significant role in astrophysics, solar sails, and energy exchange processes in high-energy environments.
