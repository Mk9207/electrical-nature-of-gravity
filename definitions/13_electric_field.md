🔹 Definition 13: Electric Field


---

■ Original Definition (Summary)

An electric field is a region in space where an electric charge exerts force on another charge. It is defined as the force experienced per unit positive charge and expressed by the following formula:

\vec{E} = \frac{\vec{F}}{q}

Where:

: Electric field vector (N/C)

: Force acting on the charge (N)

: Magnitude of the charge (C)


The electric field is a vector field, meaning it varies in magnitude and direction depending on the position. A point charge creates a radial field, while a uniformly charged plane produces a uniform field—its distribution depends on the nature of the source.
