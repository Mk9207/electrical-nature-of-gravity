# Technology 8: Utilization of Anti-Gravity Technology in Disaster Control and Mitigation

## Overview

This section discusses the application of anti-gravity module technology for disaster control and mitigation. In particular, it explores how the properties of anti-gravity can be effectively used to address various natural disasters such as **earthquakes, fires, tsunamis, and landslides**.

Unlike previous applications (Technology 5, 6, 7), this section focuses on **active control of dynamic phenomena** such as disasters.

## Main Application Structures and Implementation Concepts

1. **Earthquake Response**
   - Anti-gravity modules provide "elevated compensation" for entire buildings.
   - During crustal shifts, structures can float to a certain height to avoid horizontal displacement.
   - Modules are linked with autonomous AI that detects seismic wave intensity and triggers automatic lift mode.

2. **Fire Control and Containment**
   - Anti-gravity modules physically block rising air currents and oxygen supply during fires.
   - Spatially deployed isolation modules limit and suppress combustion zones.
   - Barriers also block ultraviolet rays from above, preventing ignition from solar sources.

3. **Tsunami and Flood Control**
   - Anti-gravity levee systems installed along coasts and rivers.
   - Upon detecting precursor waves of a tsunami, the system floats and forms a physical wall.
   - Special structures disperse water pressure to surrounding areas, ensuring durability.

4. **Landslide and Slope Collapse Measures**
   - AI analyzes signs of unstable ground and deploys floating modules above the target area.
   - Forms an "aerial deflection field" to divert falling debris or landslides.
   - In virtual simulations, the correlation between module density, placement, and slope angles has been validated.

## Advantages of Anti-Gravity Technology

- Immediate deployment, reusability, and flexibility compared to conventional physical structures.
- Even if damaged, components can be individually repaired.
- Future vision includes "aerial fortress-type disaster control systems" in conjunction with drones.

## Future Applications and Societal Implementation

- Floating domes as disaster evacuation shelters.
- Emergency restoration of infrastructure in disaster areas using connected modules.
- Preventive structures linked with virtual disaster simulations.
