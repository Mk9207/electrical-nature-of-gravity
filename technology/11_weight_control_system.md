# Technology 11: Weight Control and Mass Reduction System

## Overview

This technology focuses on reducing and controlling the "mass-related influence" of objects, aiming to solve challenges in transportation, installation, and structural durability. Based on the anti-gravity theory, it neutralizes or adjusts the gravitational pull derived from electrical properties, effectively suppressing the apparent weight of objects.

This mechanism has wide applications—from transportation to structural design and earthquake resistance—providing the following benefits:

## Structure

1. **Redistribution of Gravitational Load**
   - Multiple modules distribute load, reducing stress on any single point.
   - Particularly useful in scenarios like multi-person handling of heavy objects, enhancing both safety and efficiency.

2. **Integration with Anti-Gravity Sheets**
   - Embedding anti-gravity modules in designated areas allows automatic weight reduction for objects placed within.
   - Useful in conveyor belts, warehouse shelves, hospital beds, etc.

3. **Smart Module Configuration**
   - AI-controlled modules autonomously reposition to maintain structural integrity upon detecting risks such as collapse.
   - In seismic events, the system automatically compensates to preserve structure.

4. **Ease of Replacement and Reusability**
   - Modules are hot-swappable, allowing damaged units to be quickly replaced and restarted.
   - Older models can be adjusted and reused through standardization.

## Application Examples

- Heavy object management in factories and warehouses
- Mobility enhancement of stretchers and beds in hospitals
- Integration in building and seismic design
- Disaster mitigation through adaptive modules
- Floating exhibitions in museums and themed venues
