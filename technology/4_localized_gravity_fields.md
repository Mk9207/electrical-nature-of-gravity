# Technology 4: Localized Antigravity Field Control and Foundational Structures

## Overview

This chapter explores the foundational structure required to **localize and control antigravity effects** based on the theoretical model that defines **gravity as an electric property**. While full-scale implementations are still in the research phase, the ability to apply antigravity selectively—only where needed—is a critical objective for future applications in daily life, construction, transport, and medicine.

---

## 1. Foundational Structure and Localized Effect

- **Localized generation of antigravity fields**  
  Utilizes polarity, field intensity, and directionality to confine antigravity effects to minimal necessary areas.

- **Magnetic interference as boundary regulation**  
  Designs to mitigate leakage of antigravity fields via magnetic structures to buffer surrounding interference.

---

## 2. Boundary Design and Shielding

- **Material selection**  
  Development of rubber, ceramic, resin, carbon-based, or new insulative materials to block and contain antigravity fields.

- **Zone-based nullification**  
  Enables selective neutralization of antigravity in specific layers, preventing effects on targeted components.

---

## 3. Stabilization and Vibration Control

- **Micro-vibration fields and phase interference**  
  Ensures long-term stability of floating structures through controlled oscillations and interference fields.

- **Three-layer floating model**  
  - Top: Repulsive electric field  
  - Middle: Neutral floating object  
  - Bottom: Attractive electric field  
  Balanced upward and downward forces create floating equilibrium in experimental setups.

---

## 4. Foundational Applications

- **Residential and architectural fields**  
  - Floor-based antigravity support platforms  
  - Controlled floating furniture and equipment

- **Transport and logistics**  
  - Localized floating platforms for autonomous delivery  
  - Selective-area lifter configurations

- **Other potential uses**  
  - Non-contact transport systems in sterile medical environments  
  - Gravity-adjusted posture support systems

---

## Current Evaluation and Positioning

This structure forms a **core component for future implementation** and connects closely with subsequent technologies (Technology 5+). Emerging key development goals include:

- Boundary control techniques for antigravity field containment  
- Floating vs. anchored object design separation  
- Quantitative models for buoyancy control and safety thresholds
