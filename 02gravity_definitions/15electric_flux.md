# 🔹 Definition 15: Electric Flux

---

### ■ Original Definition (Summary)

**Electric flux** is a physical quantity representing the total amount of electric field passing through a given surface. It quantifies how much of the electric field \( \vec{E} \) penetrates a surface and is closely related to Gauss’s law.

- **Mathematical expression**:  
  \[
  \Phi_E = \int_S \vec{E} \cdot d\vec{A}
  \]  
  Where \( \Phi_E \) is the electric flux, \( \vec{E} \) is the electric field vector, and \( d\vec{A} \) is the vector surface element (with magnitude as area and direction as the outward normal).

- **Units**: Volt-meter (V·m) or Newton·meter² per Coulomb (N·m²/C)  
- When applied to a closed surface, the total flux is proportional to the net enclosed electric charge (Gauss’s Law).

This concept serves as a framework for understanding the *distributed flow* of electric fields, playing a crucial role in the spatial and visual comprehension of electrical phenomena.

---

### ■ Relevance to This Theory (Gravity = Electric Property)

In this theory, electric flux becomes a key concept for explaining the **formation and spread of gravitational fields** from an electrical perspective. Its specific roles are as follows:

#### 1. Reinterpreting Gravitational Fields as “Flux Lines”  
- If traditional gravitational fields are seen as “gravitational influence lines” radiating from objects, their density and spread resemble the mathematics of electric flux.  
- This theory interprets a body's spatial electric field distribution as constituting its “gravitational flux.”

#### 2. Flux = Gravitational Field Strength × Area  
- By multiplying the electric field emitted by a mass like Earth with the surrounding spatial surface area, we obtain a flow of force equivalent to gravity.  
- This provides a means to quantify gravitational gradients and interaction strength.

#### 3. Identifying “Gravity-Blocking Materials” via Electric Flux  
- Objects with specific structures or electrical characteristics (e.g., specialized dielectrics, polar materials) may block or reflect electric flux, thereby attenuating or modifying gravitational effects.  
- Such materials are theoretical candidates for future *gravity control materials*.

#### 4. Nonlinearity in Gravitational Response from Dynamic Electric Flux  
- If electric flux varies over time (e.g., in pulses, resonances, or interference patterns), the gravitational response in spatial pressure also becomes nonlinear.  
- This lays the foundation for reinterpreting phenomena like gravitational waves as modulated electric wave structures.

### ■ Conclusion

Electric flux offers both a visual and mathematical method for expressing the spread and strength of electric fields. Within this theory, it serves as a **framework for visualizing and quantifying spatial gravitational structures** electrically. Through this lens, the traditionally unseen nature of gravitational fields can be understood as a *measurable and structurally defined flow of force*, paving the way for deeper gravitational control and optimized design of field systems.
