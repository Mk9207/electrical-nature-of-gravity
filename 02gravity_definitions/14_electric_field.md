# Definition 14: Electric Field

## Original Definition (Key Points)

The **electric field** is a physical concept representing the force field generated by electric charges in space. It is defined at any point in space as the force experienced by a unit positive charge placed at that point and is expressed as a vector quantity.

The fundamental equation is:

\[
\vec{E} = \frac{\vec{F}}{q}
\]

- \( \vec{E} \): Electric field strength (N/C or V/m)  
- \( \vec{F} \): Force acting on the charge  
- \( q \): Magnitude of the charge

The electric field mediates the electrostatic force between charges and is widely applied in the context of **Gauss’s Law** and **Coulomb’s Law**.

## Relevance to This Theory (Gravity as an Electric Phenomenon)

In this theory, the electric field plays a central role as the **medium and manifestation of gravity**. Traditionally, gravity and electric fields have been treated as separate mediators of force, but this theory redefines them integrally as follows:

1.  
**Gravity arises from the spatial configuration of electric field strength**  
The total electric charge structure of an object forms a fine-scale electric field distribution in space. This distribution is observed not merely as electrostatic force but as a “gravitational influence.”

2.  
**Gravitational acceleration corresponds to the gradient vector of the spatial electric field**  
This theory posits that gravitational acceleration \( g \) is equivalent to the rate of change of the electric field in space (\( \nabla \vec{E} \)). Thus, it is not the distribution of mass, but the “structure of the electric field” that governs phenomena like falling and attraction.

3.  
**Gravitational waves can be interpreted as oscillatory components of the electric field**  
Rather than fluctuations in spacetime, periodic changes in spatial electric field density may be observed as gravitational waves. This opens theoretical space to interpret electromagnetic and gravitational waves as modulations of the same underlying structure.

4.  
**The Earth’s gravitational field can be redefined as a ‘composite electric field’**  
From the Earth’s core to its crust, atmosphere, and ionosphere, the total charge density and electric field interactions of all constituent matter express themselves as “total gravity.” This reconstruction enables gravity control as a technological process of “electric field reconfiguration.”

## Conclusion

In this theory, the electric field is not merely a mediator of electric forces but is regarded as **the very essence of gravity**. It unites gravity, traditionally isolated among the four fundamental forces, with electromagnetic phenomena via a shared infrastructure of the electric field, paving the way for a unified physical model.
