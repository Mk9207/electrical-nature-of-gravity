# 📘 Definition 14: Electric Field

---

## 🔹 Original Definition (Key Points)

An **electric field** is a fundamental physical construct representing the force per unit charge generated by the presence of other electric charges. It is defined by:

\[
\vec{E} = \frac{\vec{F}}{q}
\]

Where:  
- \( \vec{E} \): Electric field strength (N/C or V/m)  
- \( \vec{F} \): Force acting on a test charge  
- \( q \): Magnitude of the test charge

The electric field is a vector quantity, describing both the magnitude and direction of force at each point in space. It forms the basis of Gauss’s Law and Coulomb’s Law and underpins all electrostatic interactions.
