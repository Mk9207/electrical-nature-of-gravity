# 🔹 Definition 22: Radiative Pressure

---

## ■ Original Definition (Condensed Summary)

**Radiative pressure** refers to the force per unit area exerted by electromagnetic radiation when it transfers momentum to a material surface. It is a consequence of photon-matter interaction and is quantitatively described by:

- **P = I / c**  (for complete absorption)  
- **P = 2I / c** (for complete reflection)

Where:  
- **P** = Radiative pressure (N/m²)  
- **I** = Radiation intensity (W/m²)  
- **c** = Speed of light (≈ 3 × 10⁸ m/s)

Radiative pressure is relevant in various domains such as **astrophysics**, **plasma physics**, **laser propulsion**, and **stellar dynamics**, especially where light imparts mechanical influence on matter.
