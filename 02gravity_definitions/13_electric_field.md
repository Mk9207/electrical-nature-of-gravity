# 📘 Definition 13: Electric Field

---

## 🔹 Original Definition (Summary)

An **electric field** is a spatial region in which an electric charge experiences a force due to another charge. It is defined as the force per unit positive test charge and is represented by:

\[
\vec{E} = \frac{\vec{F}}{q}
\]

Where:  
- \( \vec{E} \): Electric field vector (N/C)  
- \( \vec{F} \): Electric force acting on the charge (N)  
- \( q \): Test charge (C)

Electric fields are vector fields and vary in both direction and magnitude depending on the spatial configuration and distribution of source charges.
