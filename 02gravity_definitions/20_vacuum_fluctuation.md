# 📘 Definition 20: Vacuum Fluctuation

---

## 🔹 Original Definition (Summary)

**Vacuum fluctuation** refers to the quantum phenomenon in which even "empty space" is not truly empty. Instead, it is a dynamic environment where:

- **Virtual particle-antiparticle pairs** spontaneously appear and annihilate.
- These processes result from the **zero-point energy** of the vacuum — the lowest energy state of quantum fields.
- Observable consequences include:
  - **Casimir effect**
  - **Vacuum polarization**
  - **Spontaneous Raman scattering shifts**
- In cosmology, vacuum fluctuations are theorized to relate to **dark energy** and **cosmic acceleration**.
