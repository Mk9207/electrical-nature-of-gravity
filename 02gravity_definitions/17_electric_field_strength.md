# 📘 Definition 17: Electric Field Strength

---

## 🔹 Original Definition (Summary)

**Electric field strength** is a vector quantity representing the force exerted per unit positive charge at a given location in space. It is defined by:

\[
\vec{E} = \frac{\vec{F}}{q}
\]

Where:  
- \( \vec{E} \): Electric field strength (V/m or N/C)  
- \( \vec{F} \): Force experienced by a charge (N)  
- \( q \): Magnitude of the charge (C)

Its connection to electric potential is given by:

\[
\vec{E} = -\nabla V
\]

This indicates that the electric field strength corresponds to the **steepness of spatial electric potential**.
