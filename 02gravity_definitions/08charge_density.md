# 📘 Definition 08: Charge Density

---

## 🔹 Original Definition

Charge density describes the amount of electric charge distributed over a given spatial region. In classical physics and electromagnetism, this is categorized into:

- **Volume charge density (ρ):** Charge per unit volume [C/m³]
- **Surface charge density (σ):** Charge per unit area [C/m²]
- **Linear charge density (λ):** Charge per unit length [C/m]

These are fundamental to calculating electric fields and potentials in electrostatic systems.
