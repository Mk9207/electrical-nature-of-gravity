
# 🧱 Proposition 02: Redefinition of Time Structure and Asymmetry

## Chapter 1: Proposition Summary

> Proposition 02: Time is a "local variable that emerges only within spatial transfer structures," and in the D structure, time observationally "disappears."

This proposition defines time not as a continuous flow, but as a local variable that only appears temporarily during spatial transitions. In the D-axis structure, time is treated as something that arises only in the transfer from A structure (black hole) to D structure (neutron star) and eventually ceases to exist.

## Chapter 2: Limitations of Traditional Time Definitions

Traditional theories (Newtonian mechanics, relativity, quantum mechanics) encounter the following limitations:

1. The origin of time cannot be determined (no definition of "before" the Big Bang)
2. Time inside black holes becomes undefined (general relativity breakdown)
3. Asymmetry between space convergence and time irreversibility
4. Contradiction between time flow and conservation laws

These stem from the misconception of time as a substantive entity.

## Chapter 3: Definition of Time in D-Axis Structure

Time is defined as a “local structural variable” that only emerges when observable changes in space occur. In static or unchanging space ("non-space"), time is undefined.

> ❌ “Changed”
> ✅ “Unfolded stored structure”

These observable changes are just outputs of previously preserved structures, not real-time alterations.
