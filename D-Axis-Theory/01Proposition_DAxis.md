## Chapter 3: Definition and Structural Order of the D-Axis

### ▍What is the A-Structure (Black Hole)?

The A-structure is a "compression structure without gravity."  
Internally, energy and charge act not through gravity but as **“transfer pressure.”**

This pressure directs all microscopic energy (electromagnetic forces, electric charges, etc.) toward the D-structure.

In this flow, the interior of the A-structure loses spatial meaning—effectively becoming **a non-space**, which appears observationally as **a negative energy region**.

Light cannot escape not because it bends, but because the **A→D transfer exceeds the speed of light**—the space itself is absorbed and transferred.

---

### ▍Why Does the Transfer End at the D-Structure?

The A→D transfer involves intermediate structures: B and C.

In space, failed nuclei or comet-like objects can interfere with this flow, leading to collisions.  
These collisions result in the following structural cascade:

| Structure | Overview | Role |
|----------|----------|------|
| **D-Structure (Neutron Star)** | Formed from the largest-scale collisions as the **most stable magnetic body** | Final destination and reconfiguration site of the transfer energy |
| **B/C Structures** | Created through medium-scale sequential collisions | Intermediate structures for smoothing and dispersing A→D flow |
| **A-Structure** | Fusion reactions generate high-density energy at final collision | Acts as the compression source seeking output toward D |

Thus, the D-structure is the **"exit" of the transfer structure**, and what is observed as the **"beginning" (Big Bang)**.

The D-axis structure reverses the conventional definition that the universe began with the Big Bang,  
and redefines the **Big Bang as the dispersive phenomenon caused by the fusion of the A-structure (black hole) and D-structure (neutron star).**

1. Material transferable into the A-structure is exhausted, and compression pressure decreases

5. The resulting release = **dispersal**, observed as the **Big Bang**

This acceleration—filling the void—is what is **observed as dark energy driving cosmic expansion**.
